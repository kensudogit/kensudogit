## Governance Rules

1. Keep S limited to a small set of clearly differentiated flagship projects.
2. Promote B to A only after README, architecture, security, and runnable/demo value are clear.
3. Move superseded repositories to C and link to the maintained successor before archiving.
4. Do not archive repositories solely because they are old or large; assess differentiation and retained technical value.
